## Bundled fonts

`assets/fonts/` contains Liberation Sans (Regular, Bold, Italic, Bold Italic), version 2.1.5, from <https://github.com/liberationfonts/liberation-fonts>. It is licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1 (see `assets/fonts/LICENSE-LiberationSans-OFL.txt`) and is not covered by the proprietary notice above. It is metric-compatible with Arial and is bundled so PDFs build identically on every machine. Do not add proprietary fonts (Arial, Segoe UI, ...) to this directory. The body font (Libertinus Serif) is embedded in the Typst binary (SIL OFL 1.1).
